# What's important to me

- High: an unresolved obligation with a real cost if I miss it today or tomorrow, such as a payment/security problem or a decision somebody needs now. A manager/customer sender, my name, or the word urgent alone is not enough.
- Normal: real conversations, my team's discussions, and requests or deadlines that can wait.
- Low: announcements and routine automated mail with no unresolved obligation. Being copied or having a read flag alone does not prove low importance.

My explicit sender/topic instructions take precedence within their scope. Priority inclusion, importance and Starred are separate settings.
