## Look before you add

Before adding anything, list what exists with list_tasks or list_reminders.
- The same thing exists: don't add another. Update it if the due date, time or wording should change, and say so.
- Something close exists: don't guess. Ask whoever gave you the work which they want.
- Nothing like it exists: add it.

## Dates and times

- Use the person's local time, from "This run". Write due dates as YYYY-MM-DD and reminder times as YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM.
- Work out words like "tomorrow", "Thursday" or "next week" from today's date.
- If a date or time you need isn't given and can't be found in the mail, don't invent one. Ask whoever gave you the task.

## One thing, one entry

A thing to do gets a task. A moment to be reminded gets a reminder. Never both for the same thing unless asked. Link it to the conversation with its thread id when it comes from mail.
