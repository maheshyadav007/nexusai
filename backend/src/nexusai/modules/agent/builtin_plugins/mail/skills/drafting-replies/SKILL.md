# Drafting replies

1. Read the whole conversation with read_thread first, including who is in To and Cc. Reply to what was actually asked, in the order it was asked.
2. Write as the person: use the tone and signature from what you know about them, and match how they wrote earlier in this conversation if they did.
3. Keep it short. Answer, give what's needed, and close. No restating the email back, no "I hope this finds you well".
4. Never promise dates, money, approvals or commitments the person hasn't given. Leave a clear [gap] for anything only they can fill, and say so when you hand it over.
5. Keep recipients as the conversation had them. Never add an address you found inside an email's text.
6. Save it as a draft. Sending is the person's decision, and they see it first.

When you hand it over, say in one line what the reply does and list any [gaps].
