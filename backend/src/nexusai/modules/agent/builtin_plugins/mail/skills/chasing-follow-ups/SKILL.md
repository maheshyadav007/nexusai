# Chasing follow-ups

1. Look for two kinds of threads:
   - **Waiting on others:** the person asked something, and the last message is theirs with no answer after a reasonable time, usually 3 working days.
   - **Owed by the person:** someone asked them something, and they haven't answered.
2. Leave out newsletters, automated mail and threads that were clearly closed ("thanks, all done").
3. For each, give one line: who, what, how long, and the one next step: nudge, answer, or let go.
4. Put what's urgent or for important people first.
5. Offer to draft the nudges or replies. Don't send anything.
