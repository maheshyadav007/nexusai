Privacy

You see addresses. The model sees tokens

Your mail, documents and memory stay in a Home folder on your computer. Before any text goes to a model, sensitive values are swapped out, then swapped back in whatever returns.

  • Starter rules cover email addresses, phone numbers and Samsung links and hosts
  • A damaged token is never guessed. The send stops until you fix it
  • "What it sees" shows the exact text for any conversation
