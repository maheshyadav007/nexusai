# Posty gaze mechanics

The envelope is a flexible paper body with a printed face. Keep feet, lower envelope corners, size and ground contact anchored. The two square eye surfaces shift together slightly toward the requested direction on the upper envelope face. Their original charcoal square pupils also aim within the ivory-white squares. A small flex of the upper envelope follows the face direction, without turning away or rotating the whole sprite. Both eyes remain visible for all directions, envelope flap seams remain intact, arms remain attached. Use strong upward/downward pupil and face-group displacement for vertical cardinals, and strong left/right displacement for horizontal cardinals. Diagonals must carry both axes. Intermediates change evenly around the loop without changes in eye design, palette or body size.
